# Wallet-Integrity-Scanner
Wallet Integrity Scanner
A professional tool for independent security auditing of cryptocurrency wallets.

Wallet Integrity Scanner is an open-source tool designed for crypto enthusiasts, investors, and developers. Its purpose is to help you proactively check the security of your wallets by identifying potential vulnerabilities and signs of compromise before attackers do.

We believe that the best defense is prevention. Our scanner gives you the ability to take control of the security of your assets.

Key features
Seed phrase (mnemonic phrase) integrity check: Analyzes the structure and entropy of your phrase for compliance with BIP39 standards, identifying common weaknesses.

Private key audit: Helps detect keys generated with insufficient randomness or potentially compromised in known incidents.

Public address monitoring: Checks the transaction history of your addresses for suspicious activity, links to hacker wallets, or mixer services.

Security practices assessment: Checks whether your addresses have been used in public spaces (social networks, GitHub), which could have led to data leaks.

Local and confidential analysis: ALL CHECKS are performed locally on your device. Your seed phrases and private keys never leave your computer. We only use public data from the blockchain.

Who is this tool for?
Crypto investors who want to verify the reliability of their cold and hot wallets.

DeFi and NFT users whose wallets actively interact with multiple smart contracts.

Blockchain project developers conducting internal security audits.

Cryptocurrency beginners who want to learn the basics of cryptographic security in practice.

Owners of old wallets who want to check their status after many years.

Important warning and principles of ethical use
This project is strictly educational and intended for self-auditing.

DO NOT use this tool to check wallets that do not belong to you. This is illegal and unethical.

This tool is NOT a keylogger, stealer, or hacking software. Its purpose is protection, not attack.

We are NOT responsible for any loss of funds due to misuse of the program, including entering seed phrases into phishing sites.

Use the tool ONLY to check your own seed phrase backups or wallets created in an isolated environment.
Always follow basic security rules: use hardware wallets, store your seed phrase offline, and enable two-factor authentication.
